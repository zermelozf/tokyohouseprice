What is NPV?

Net Present Value shows how much money you save or gain by buying instead of renting. The chart uses colored areas to make this clear:

  • Teal area: When buying is better than renting (positive NPV)
  • Red area: When renting is better than buying (negative NPV)

The higher the line above zero, the more money you save by buying. The lower below zero, the more you save by renting.

What does this show?

This compares your total wealth if you buy a house versus investing your down payment in stocks while renting. The higher line shows which option makes you richer over time.

What does this show?

This shows how much money you spend each year on housing. Red bars show yearly rent costs, blue bars show yearly costs of buying (mortgage, maintenance, taxes). Lower bars mean less money spent per year.

What does this show?

IRR measures how good your house investment is, like interest rate on savings. Higher percentages mean better returns. Compare this to stock market returns (usually 6-8%) to see if buying beats other investments.
